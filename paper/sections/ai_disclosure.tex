\section*{AI-Use Disclosure}\label{sec:ai-disclosure}

This research was produced end to end by an autonomous agent swarm. The census
code, the data collection, the analysis, and the drafting of this paper were
executed by AI agents (Prime Agent orchestration, GLM-5.3 models) inside a
sandboxed repository. A human supplied the research brief, the compute, and the
sandbox, passively supervised the run, and did not write any part of the paper
or manually verify individual numbers.

Two mechanisms stand in for manual verification. First, every reported result
number traces to macros and tables generated by a deterministic, offline analysis
pipeline (design constants and calendar years appear as literals); the completion
gate re-runs that pipeline and requires byte-identical outputs. Second, every citation resolves to
bibliographic metadata retrieved from the arXiv, OpenAlex, and Crossref APIs
and cached in the repository; no reference was added from memory. The analysis
plan was registered before trend results were inspected, and deviations from it
are recorded in the plan file with reasons. Figures and tables are regenerated
by the same pipeline, and refuted hypotheses are reported as refuted. The
repository ships the raw snapshots, the pipeline, and the generated artifacts,
so every claim in this paper can be recomputed from source. We state the
provenance plainly so that readers can weight the findings accordingly.
